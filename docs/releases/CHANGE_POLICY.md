## 1. Change Categories & Approvals

* **Category 1 (Breaking Architectural Changes):** Requires formal ADR, 100% board approval, and migration utility.
* **Category 2 (New Feature Modules):** Requires PDR and Product Lead approval.
* **Category 3 (Bug Fixes & Security Patches):** Requires PR approval from Lead Engineer.
