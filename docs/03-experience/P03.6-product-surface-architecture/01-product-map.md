## 2. Product Hierarchy & Module Clusters

The visible product surface is organized into 5 major product clusters:
1. **Core Operating Surface:** Command Center, Home Experience, Executive Brief Queue.
2. **Execution & Workspaces:** Mission Engine, Projects, Sovereign Knowledge Vaults.
3. **Synthetic Staff & Intelligence:** AI Employee Charters, Departmental Skills, Memory Graph.
4. **Business Operations Modules:** CRM, Sales, Finance, HR, Marketing, Analytics, Connectors.
5. **Platform & Governance:** Administration, Marketplace, Developer Platform, THEKY Control Center.
