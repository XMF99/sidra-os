## 1. Primary Persona Specifications

### 1. Persona: The Sovereign Founder / CEO
* **Goals:** Operate entire company with maximum cognitive leverage and minimal headcount drag.
* **Daily Workflow:** Launches THEKY ➔ Reviews Executive Brief Queue (`Cmd+B`) ➔ Issues 1-click sign-offs ➔ Declares new strategic intents (`Cmd+K`).
* **Primary Modules:** Home, Missions, CRM, Sales, Finance, AI Employees.
* **AI Usage:** Delegates multi-department missions to Synthetic Executive Coordinators.
* **Permissions:** Workspace Super-Admin; Multi-Sig Authority (**INV-08**).

### 2. Persona: The Enterprise Chief Architect / CTO
* **Goals:** Maintain zero-trust security boundaries, enforce ADR compliance, manage developer platform.
* **Daily Workflow:** Inspects ADR compliance reports ➔ Monitors AIOps model routing proxy ➔ Audits agent security fences.
* **Primary Modules:** Knowledge, Administration, Developer, Connectors, Security.
* **AI Usage:** Employs Independent Security and Architecture Reviewer agents.
