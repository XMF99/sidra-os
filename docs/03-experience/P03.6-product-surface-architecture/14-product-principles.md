## 1. The Nine Immutable Product Surface Principles

1. **Principle 1 (Mission-First Execution):** Work is organized into goal-oriented missions, never ephemeral chat transcripts.
2. **Principle 2 (Workspace-First Sovereignty):** The local encrypted workspace vault is the primary product (**INV-04**).
3. **Principle 3 (AI as Synthetic Collaborator):** AI agents operate under delegated charters with explicit capability fences.
4. **Principle 4 (Human Authority Supremacy):** Strategic sign-off and approval belong uniquely to the Human Principal (**INV-01**).
5. **Principle 5 (Conversation is Secondary):** Streaming chat is an emergency fallback; consolidated briefs are the primary interface.
6. **Principle 6 (Everything Searchable):** All documents, memory graphs, and commands are discoverable in <50ms (**INV-06**).
7. **Principle 7 (Everything Contextual):** Context is automatically injected from the local organizational graph.
8. **Principle 8 (Everything Extensible):** Open SDKs power a 70/30 developer marketplace ecosystem.
9. **Principle 9 (Local-First Priority):** 100% core capability functions offline without cloud network dependencies.
