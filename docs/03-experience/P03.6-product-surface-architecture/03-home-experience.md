## 1. Information Architecture of Conceptual Home

The Home Experience in THEKY is the **Executive Flight Deck**. It is designed around information density and 1-click strategic sign-offs.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                        FIVE CONCEPTUAL HOME INFORMATION BLOCKS                    |
|                                                                                   |
|  1. Executive Brief Queue  ── Max 3 active decisions requiring Principal sign-off.|
|  2. Daily Priority Stream  ── Strategic goals, active milestone status.           |
|  3. Mission Queue          ── Live DAG execution progress across AI departments. |
|  4. AI Recommendations     ── Risk flags, cost optimization, memory graph hits.|
|  5. Recent Activity Ledger ── Hash-chained audit stream of completed actions.    |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

## 2. Attention Reverence Rules (INV-01)

Home never displays red unread badges, flashing banners, or infinite social feeds. Information is structured strictly by **Priority & Decision Necessity**.
