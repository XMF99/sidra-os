## 1. Universal Accessibility Standards

1. **100% Keyboard Operability:** Every action, command, brief sign-off, and document traversal is fully operable without mouse interaction.
2. **Screen Reader Semantics:** Structural ARIA landmarks provide clear screen reader navigation across executive briefs.
3. **Native RTL / LTR Localization:** Native support for Arabic (RTL) and English (LTR) language structures.
4. **Low-Bandwidth & Offline Resilience:** Full core functionality remains operable offline or on high-latency networks (**INV-04**).
