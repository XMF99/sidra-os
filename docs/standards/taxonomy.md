## 1. Documentation Category Taxonomy

All knowledge artifacts in the repository belong to one of 6 official documentation categories:

```
Category 1: Constitutional Governance (`docs/00-constitution/`) ── Permanent inviolable charter
Category 2: Strategy & Economics (`docs/01-strategy/`)         ── Market, business model, & roadmap
Category 3: Core Architecture (`docs/02-architecture/`)         ── Invariants, domains, & compute
Category 4: Experience & Workflows (`docs/03-experience/`)       ── Navigation, lifecycles, & IA
Category 5: Operations & Governance (`docs/05-operations/`)     ── Enterprise fleet & multi-principal
Category 6: AI Platform (`docs/06-ai/`)                         ── Protocols, personas, & safety policies
```
