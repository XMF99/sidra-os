## 3. Customer Segment Typology & Value Drivers

### 3.1 Solo Founder & Sovereign Creator
* **Profile:** 1-person companies building high-revenue technology products or creative studios.
* **Economic Pain Point:** Cannot afford $500k in early employee salaries; spends 60% of time managing freelancers and context-switching.
* **Willingness to Pay:** High elasticity for tools that directly yield shipping velocity ($30 - $100/mo).

### 3.2 AI-Native Startup (2 – 15 Employees)
* **Profile:** Seed to Series A software companies aiming to operate with the output of a 100-person firm.
* **Economic Pain Point:** Meeting drag, requirement fragmentation, unverified AI code/marketing drafts.
* **Willingness to Pay:** $50 - $150 / user / month.

### 3.3 High-Growth Tech SMB (15 – 100 Employees)
* **Profile:** Fast-growing engineering and product teams managing complex multi-department workflows.
* **Economic Pain Point:** Architectural drift, context loss across Slack/Jira, high management overhead.
* **Willingness to Pay:** $100 - $250 / user / month.

### 3.4 Mid-Market Enterprise (100 – 1,000 Employees)
* **Profile:** Established technology, financial, and digital service firms with strict data governance needs.
* **Economic Pain Point:** Security compliance risks with cloud AI, high SaaS tool sprawl.
* **Willingness to Pay:** $20k - $100k / year annual enterprise agreements.

### 3.5 Sovereign Enterprise & Government / Defense (1,000+ Employees)
* **Profile:** Defense, healthcare, fintech, and sovereign state entities demanding 100% air-gapped data sovereignty.
* **Economic Pain Point:** Absolute prohibition against third-party multi-tenant cloud storage for sensitive intelligence.
* **Willingness to Pay:** $250k - $2M+ / year custom sovereign contracts.

## 5. AI Pricing & Orchestration Strategy

### 5.1 Hybrid Monetization Model
THEKY decouples desktop application licensing from AI compute consumption to maintain high gross margins.

```
[ User Input Intent ]
          │
          ▼
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│             THEKY Intelligent Model Router             │
└──────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────┘
                       │
         ┌─────────────┴─────────────┐
         ▼                           ▼
┌──────────────────┐       ┌────────────────────────────┐
│  Local Model     │       │  Cloud Frontier Model      │
│  (Zero Compute   │       │  (OpenAI / Anthropic /     │
│   Cost to THEKY) │       │   THEKY Sovereign Relay)   │
└──────────────────┘       └─────────────┬──────────────┘
                                         │
                                         ▼
                           ┌────────────────────────────┐
                           │ 25 - 35% Orchestration     │
                           │ Margin Retained by THEKY   │
                           └────────────────────────────┘
```

### 5.2 AI Tier Breakdown & Why
1. **Bring Your Own Key (BYOK):** Available on Free & Starter tiers. Users plug in their own API keys; THEKY charges zero markup on local execution.
2. **Included Monthly Credits:** Paid plans bundle base credit allocations (e.g., Professional includes 5,000 credits/mo), driving predictable MRR.
3. **Pay-As-You-Go Overages:** Metered usage above base credits billed at cost + 30% margin.
4. **Intelligent Model Routing:** Low-complexity task steps (formatting, parsing) are routed to fast local models (Olama/Llama) or cheap API tiers. High-stakes strategic tasks route to frontier models (Claude 3.5 Sonnet, GPT-4o), optimizing customer credit efficiency while preserving THEKY's gross margin.
5. **Private Sovereign Cloud Compute:** Enterprise clients purchase dedicated GPU compute clusters hosted in air-gapped sovereign regions.
