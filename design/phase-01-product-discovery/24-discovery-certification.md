### Identified Observations & Inconsistencies:
1. **Legacy Naming Artifacts:** Documents `01` through `17` were authored during initial discovery under the working title *Sidra OS*. Document `18` (Brand Architecture) explicitly established **THEKY OS** as the final master brand name and designated "Sidra" as a forbidden legacy code name. While document `18` governs, Phase 2 documentation must strictly use `THEKY OS`.
2. **Pricing Tier Evolution:** `07-market.md` (initial discovery) estimated a lower pricing baseline than `19-business-model.md` (final business model). Document `19` represents the final, authoritative financial model ($29 Starter / $79 Professional / $199 Business / Enterprise ELAs).

## 4. Identified Gaps & Remaining Operational Risks

The board highlights 3 operational edge cases that must be addressed during Phase 2 Product Strategy:

1. **Local Neural Accelerator Heterogeneity:** Hardware performance varies widely across Apple Silicon M-series, NVIDIA RTX, and Intel NPUs. Phase 2 must specify dynamic local model quantization selection based on detected VRAM/NPU limits.
2. **Cross-Border Regulatory Conflict Resolution:** If an enterprise operates across US and EU jurisdictions, conflicts between US discovery subpoenas and EU GDPR right-to-be-forgotten deletion rules must be resolved within the data retention policy.
3. **Third-Party Agent Marketplace Malware Liability:** The 70/30 developer marketplace (`23-owner-control-center.md`) requires automated static analysis pipelines to prevent malicious prompt injection or data exfiltration inside third-party agent packs.
