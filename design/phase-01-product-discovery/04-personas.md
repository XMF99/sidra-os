# Sidra OS — Primary Personas & User Profiles

> **Phase 01: Product Discovery**  
> **Document:** 04-personas.md  
> **Status:** Approved Strategy  

---

## Overview

Sidra OS serves 12 distinct organizational roles within modern technology enterprises, high-growth startups, and sovereign creative studios. Whether these roles are held by individual human professionals or supercharged by a human Principal managing synthetic departments, Sidra OS provides tailored workflows for each.

---

## 1. The Founder

* **Role Overview:** Early-stage visionary managing product vision, fundraising, initial strategy, hiring, and company survival.
* **Key Motivations:** Maximizing enterprise velocity, conserving runway, moving from idea to market validation in record time.
* **Primary Sidra OS Workflows:** 
  * High-level Intent Statements ("Launch MVP of feature X by Friday").
  * Executive Brief review and strategic sign-offs.
  * Cross-department delegation (Product -> Engineering -> Marketing -> Legal).
* **Mental Model:** "I want to declare what our company will build today and receive audited, launch-ready results tomorrow without managing 10 contractors."
* **Primary Interaction Style:** Keyboard-driven executive Command Bar, high-level strategy briefs, capital/budget allocation knobs.

---

## 2. Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

* **Role Overview:** Leader responsible for corporate strategy, resource allocation, organizational alignment, board relations, and top-line growth.
* **Key Motivations:** Clear operational visibility, risk mitigation, alignment across departments, fast strategic execution.
* **Primary Sidra OS Workflows:**
  * Multi-department health check briefs (Finance, Sales, Product, Engineering).
  * Strategic decision records (ADR/ODR approvals).
  * High-stakes external brief approvals (investor memos, press releases).
* **Mental Model:** "Show me the top 3 company risks today, the status of our quarterly initiatives, and give me actionable decision briefs."
* **Primary Interaction Style:** Daily Executive Briefing interface, decision queue, macro KPI health monitor.

---

## 3. Chief Operating Officer (COO)

* **Role Overview:** Operations leader ensuring cross-departmental execution, process optimization, resource efficiency, and project delivery.
* **Key Motivations:** Eliminating process bottlenecks, enforcing organizational standards, ensuring auditability and compliance.
* **Primary Sidra OS Workflows:**
  * Workflow bottleneck identification & department throughput review.
  * Capability fence and permission budget management across agent teams.
  * Cross-functional project dependency tracking.
* **Mental Model:** "I need frictionless operational execution across departments with zero communication leaks and absolute compliance auditability."
* **Primary Interaction Style:** Process topology maps, department allocation dashboards, fence and security rule managers.

---

## 4. Chief Technology Officer (CTO)

* **Role Overview:** Technical leader directing system architecture, engineering standards, infrastructure reliability, and technical strategy.
* **Key Motivations:** Technical excellence, zero architectural debt, deterministic builds, strict security sandboxing, fast shipping.
* **Primary Sidra OS Workflows:**
  * Architectural Decision Records (ADR) review and approval.
  * Code quality & security audit brief inspection from synthetic engineering departments.
  * Infrastructure deployment budget and capability configuration.
* **Mental Model:** "I want synthetic engineering departments to write pristine, tested code that strictly adheres to our architecture rules without hallucinating security risks."
* **Primary Interaction Style:** Code audit review panels, architecture diff viewers, repository memory graph inspections.

---

## 5. Project Manager / Product Manager (PM)

* **Role Overview:** Product lead defining specs, user stories, sprint priorities, cross-functional roadmaps, and delivery schedules.
* **Key Motivations:** High shipping velocity, crystal-clear PRDs, zero requirement gaps, seamless handoffs between design, dev, and QA.
* **Primary Sidra OS Workflows:**
  * Spec & PRD generation via Synthetic Product Department.
  * Automatic feature decomposition into execution graphs.
  * Sprint verification & release brief validation.
* **Mental Model:** "Transform customer feedback and product vision into unambiguous, fully validated engineering task briefs instantly."
* **Primary Interaction Style:** Task dependency graphs, PRD review interfaces, feature release changelog generators.

---

## 6. Developer / Software Engineer

* **Role Overview:** Core builder writing system logic, APIs, frontend components, tests, and managing CI/CD pipelines.
* **Key Motivations:** Uninterrupted flow state, high-quality APIs, clear code specifications, zero tedious boilerplate writing, rapid debugging.
* **Primary Sidra OS Workflows:**
  * Local repository indexing & code context queries.
  * Synthetic engineering agent task delegation (test suite generation, refactoring, doc writing).
  * Code review and pull request audit briefs.
* **Mental Model:** "Let me stay in my code flow while synthetic engineering departments draft tests, handle boilerplate, and verify safety."
* **Primary Interaction Style:** Code diff review, terminal-integrated command palette, local repository index inspector.

---

## 7. Product / UX Designer

* **Role Overview:** Visual and interaction designer crafting user journeys, design systems, design specs, and interactive prototypes.
* **Key Motivations:** Cohesive aesthetics, pixel-perfect design system token adherence, intuitive UX workflows, accessible interfaces.
* **Primary Sidra OS Workflows:**
  * User flow & information architecture spec generation.
  * Design system rule enforcement across synthetic copy and frontend tasks.
  * Design audit review briefs for new feature workflows.
* **Mental Model:** "Define non-negotiable UX laws and design system tokens, then let the system verify that every workflow respects them."
* **Primary Interaction Style:** Workflow step inspectors, spatial canvas overview, design token reference tables.

---

## 8. Finance Lead / CFO

* **Role Overview:** Financial steward managing burn rate, departmental budgets, financial forecasting, payroll, and invoice processing.
* **Key Motivations:** Financial accuracy, budget compliance, runway extension, zero surprise expenditures, automated audit trails.
* **Primary Sidra OS Workflows:**
  * Token and cloud infrastructure cost budget enforcement per department.
  * Financial model generation and scenario stress-testing briefs.
  * Vendor expense audit and invoice reconciliation.
* **Mental Model:** "Every synthetic department has a hard financial ceiling. Give me real-time cost visibility and mathematical precision."
* **Primary Interaction Style:** Ledger transaction view, budget ceiling dial controls, financial report summaries.

---

## 9. Marketing Lead / CMO

* **Role Overview:** Growth driver managing brand positioning, content distribution, product launches, SEO, and demand generation.
* **Key Motivations:** Compelling messaging, consistent brand voice, rapid campaign execution, high conversion rates.
* **Primary Sidra OS Workflows:**
  * Product launch brief generation across launch channels.
  * Synthetic content strategy creation with brand voice constraint enforcement.
  * Competitive messaging analysis and market positioning briefs.
* **Mental Model:** "Turn our product updates into high-impact launch campaigns across all channels with 100% brand voice consistency."
* **Primary Interaction Style:** Campaign matrix view, brand guideline inspector, content diff approval card.

---

## 10. Human Resources (HR) / People Lead

* **Role Overview:** Talent strategist managing talent onboarding, organizational structure, performance standards, and company culture.
* **Key Motivations:** Smooth onboarding workflows, clear role charters, employee satisfaction, compliance with labor standards.
* **Primary Sidra OS Workflows:**
  * Synthetic agent department charter definitions & role responsibilities.
  * Team onboarding knowledge package creation.
  * Department performance & velocity review briefs.
* **Mental Model:** "Define exact boundaries, charters, and expectations for every role in the enterprise—human or synthetic."
* **Primary Interaction Style:** Department org-chart view, charter editor, role capability matrix.

---

## 11. Sales Lead / Head of Revenue

* **Role Overview:** Revenue generator managing lead qualification, enterprise deal pipeline, client proposals, and sales ops.
* **Key Motivations:** High conversion velocity, deep buyer discovery context, rapid tailored pitch deck & RFP responses, reliable deal closures.
* **Primary Sidra OS Workflows:**
  * Enterprise prospect research & deal brief generation.
  * RFP response drafting with synthetic legal and technical review.
  * Deal pipeline health check & revenue projection briefs.
* **Mental Model:** "Give me instant strategic briefs on enterprise accounts with technical feasibility answers validated by engineering."
* **Primary Interaction Style:** Deal brief queue, prospect intelligence cards, revenue forecast views.

---

## 12. Customer Support Lead / Head of Success

* **Role Overview:** Customer champion managing support workflows, issue resolution, customer feedback loops, and satisfaction metrics.
* **Key Motivations:** Lightning-fast issue resolution, customer retention, root-cause bug reporting directly to engineering.
* **Primary Sidra OS Workflows:**
  * Support incident aggregation and root-cause analysis briefs.
  * Direct bug brief handoff to synthetic engineering teams.
  * Knowledge base article update generation based on solved tickets.
* **Mental Model:** "Automatically trace customer support friction to root engineering issues and update documentation instantly."
* **Primary Interaction Style:** Incident root-cause stream, customer sentiment monitor, docs update approval card.
