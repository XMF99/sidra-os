## Detailed Category Evaluations

### 1. Product Vision — 9.5 / 10
* **Verdict:** Exceptional. The paradigm shift from "pages/dashboards to execute" to "synthetic departments that execute" is groundbreaking. Rejecting chat windows is a bold, highly defensible stance.

### 2. Strategic Mission — 9.0 / 10
* **Verdict:** Excellent. Clear mission statement with explicit boundaries defining what Sidra OS IS and IS NOT.

### 3. Product Principles — 9.5 / 10
* **Verdict:** Outstanding. The 10 ranked invariants (P1 Attention > P2 Delegation > P7 Sovereignty) provide crystal-clear decision hierarchy for future engineering and design phases.

### 4. Target Market — 7.5 / 10
* **Verdict:** Strong segmentation across 4 enterprise typologies, but TAM/SAM/SOM estimates lack bottoms-up validation.

### 5. Primary Personas — 8.5 / 10
* **Verdict:** Comprehensive 12-persona breakdown. Minor deduction for omitting the IT Systems Administrator persona.

### 6. Daily Jobs To Be Done (JTBD) — 9.0 / 10
* **Verdict:** Rigorous "When / Want to / So that" formulations and daily cadences across all personas.

### 7. Competitive Analysis — 8.5 / 10
* **Verdict:** Sharp critique of Notion, Linear, ClickUp, and Cursor. Clear identification of 4 core strategic moats.

### 8. North Star Experience — 9.5 / 10
* **Verdict:** Compelling, vivid workday narrative and visionary 2030 strategic horizon.

### 9. Business & Commercial Strategy — 4.0 / 10 (CRITICAL DEFECT)
* **Verdict:** Unacceptable for a multi-billion-dollar strategy. Omits pricing tiers, monetization models, and unit economics entirely.

### 10. AI Strategy — 9.5 / 10
* **Verdict:** World-class. "Delegation Over Prompting" and "Separation of Powers" set a new industry benchmark for high-trust AI UX.

### 11. Enterprise Readiness — 4.5 / 10 (CRITICAL DEFECT)
* **Verdict:** Deficient. Lacks IT admin fleet management, SAML/SSO integration, SIEM audit streaming, and SOC 2 compliance paths.
