## 1. Target Market Definition

Sidra OS targets the rapidly emerging market of **AI-Native Enterprises, High-Leverage Lean Technology Startups, and Sovereign Sovereign Knowledge Studios**.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                             MARKET SIZE ESTIMATION                            |
|                                                                               |
|  TAM: $120B+ (Global Enterprise Productivity, Work Management, & AI Tools)   |
|  SAM: $28B (AI-Native Tech Companies, Software Labs, & High-Growth Startups)  |
|  SOM: $4.5B (Sovereign Local-First Enterprises & High-Leverage Founders/CTOs) |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 1.1 Total Addressable Market (TAM) — $120B+
The global market for enterprise collaboration, work management, developer tooling, project management software, and enterprise AI automation services.

### 1.2 Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) — $28B
Technology-forward organizations (software companies, AI labs, fintechs, creative technology agencies, venture-backed startups) that actively utilize advanced AI tooling, care about deep engineering velocity, and spend heavily on modern software stacks (Cursor, Linear, Notion, Vercel).

### 1.3 Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) — $4.5B
High-leverage founders, executive leaders, CTOs, and sovereign enterprises demanding:
* Local-first data sovereignty (healthcare, fintech, defense, IP-sensitive labs).
* High-trust autonomous execution over simple chat assistants.
* Radical reduction in organizational management overhead.

## 2. Target Company Profiles & Typology

Sidra OS is engineered specifically for four primary enterprise profiles.

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                         TARGET COMPANY PROFILE TYPOLOGY                         |
|                                                                                 |
|  1. The "10x Lean" AI-Native Startup  3. Sovereign IP-Sensitive Enterprise      |
|  2. High-Growth Tech Scale-Up        4. High-Value Agency / Sovereign Studio  |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 2.1 Profile A: The "10x Lean" AI-Native Startup
* **Company Size:** 1 to 15 employees.
* **Capitalization:** Seed to Series A ($1M - $10M raised).
* **Characteristics:** High ambition, extremely small human headcount, heavy reliance on AI tools. Founder acts as CEO, product manager, and lead strategist.
* **Core Need:** Wants to operate with the output of a 100-person company without hiring mid-level management or burning cash on heavy ops.
* **Why Sidra OS:** Acts as the complete synthetic executive and departmental execution layer.

---

### 2.2 Profile B: The High-Growth Tech Scale-Up
* **Company Size:** 20 to 150 employees.
* **Capitalization:** Series A to Series C.
* **Characteristics:** Fast product shipping, engineering-heavy culture, complex cross-department dependencies (Engineering, Product, Marketing, Legal, Finance).
* **Core Need:** Reclaim engineering velocity, eliminate meeting friction, enforce strict architecture standards across fast-growing teams.
* **Why Sidra OS:** Replaces fragmented task boards with crisp, audited briefs and departmental execution graphs.

---

### 2.3 Profile C: Sovereign IP-Sensitive Enterprise
* **Company Size:** 50 to 500+ employees.
* **Sectors:** Fintech, Biotech, HealthTech, Defense, Cybersecurity, Proprietary Trading.
* **Characteristics:** Strict regulatory oversight (SOC2, HIPAA, GDPR), highly sensitive intellectual property, zero tolerance for cloud data leaks.
* **Core Need:** Advanced AI-driven departmental automation without allowing enterprise data to reside in third-party multi-tenant cloud storage.
* **Why Sidra OS:** Local-first architecture guarantees that all vector indices, memory graphs, and event logs remain strictly on local encrypted disk.

---

### 2.4 Profile D: High-Value Agency / Sovereign Studio
* **Company Size:** 5 to 30 employees.
* **Business Model:** Bespoke software development, executive design, financial advisory, specialized consulting.
* **Characteristics:** High billable rates, multi-client context management, high-touch client deliverables.
* **Core Need:** Deliver client work with 10x speed, automatically compile client intelligence briefs, maintain complete audit trails for billable work.
* **Why Sidra OS:** Provides isolated client workspaces with autonomous department execution for research, drafting, and code audits.
