## 1. Approval Outcome & Rationale

Phase 01: Product Discovery is formally **APPROVED WITH REQUIRED CHANGES**.

### Why It Is Approved:
The foundational product philosophy of Sidra OS represents a category-defining leap in desktop software and enterprise AI execution. The strategic core—specifically the rejection of chat interfaces in favor of intention-driven delegation, the independent reviewer audit mechanism, and the local-first sovereign data architecture—is fundamentally sound, brilliant, and fully capable of supporting a multi-billion-dollar enterprise operating system.

The following core decisions are **FROZEN**. Future design, workflow, architecture, and implementation phases are **STRICTLY FORBIDDEN** from altering, diluting, or reversing these foundational commitments:

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

---

## 3. What Must Remain Stable Throughout All Future Phases

1. **The Interaction Paradigm:** The primary interaction model must remain intention-driven delegation resulting in consolidated executive briefs, NOT conversational chat streams.
2. **The Data Sovereignty Model:** Workspace memory, event ledgers, and document artifacts must remain stored locally on user hardware in open, standard formats (Markdown/JSONL).
3. **The Ten Ranked Product Principles:** The order of principles defined in `03-principles.md` (where Principle 1 strictly overrides Principle 2, Principle 2 overrides Principle 3, etc.) must govern all future design trade-offs.
4. **The Audit Trust Mechanism:** Every automated departmental output must pass independent reviewer verification before presentation to the human Principal.
