## 1. Permanent Design Freeze Mandate

1. **Absolute Design Lock:** All UI specifications, screen architectures, wireframes, enterprise design systems, component libraries, responsive viewports, and interaction specs certified across Programs P05 through P08.6 are **PERMANENTLY FROZEN**.
