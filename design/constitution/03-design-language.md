## 1. Experience Language Governance

This document establishes the 10 core experience attributes defining the visual, tactile, and interaction feel of **THEKY OS** and **THEKY Control Center**. Every future UX spec, design system component, typography choice, and interaction motion must comply with this experience language.

---

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                    THE TEN EXPERIENCE ATTRIBUTES                                        |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  1. Executive Calm        ── Quiet obsidian aesthetics protecting cognitive focus.                     |
|  2. Uncompromising Speed   ── Sub-50ms desktop latency on every action (INV-06).                       |

```

## 3. Experience Rules for Future UX/UI Design

1. **Rule 1 (Obsidian Quietude):** Interfaces default to low-luminance dark obsidian palettes to prevent eye fatigue during deep work.
2. **Rule 2 (Semantic Color Only):** Color is forbidden as decoration. Color is applied strictly for state semantics (Audited Emerald = Pass; Sovereign Amber = Uncertainty; Crimson = Fence Breach).
3. **Rule 3 (Keyboard Command Primacy):** `Cmd+K` is always accessible from any view in under 15ms.
