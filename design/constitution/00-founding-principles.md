### Principle 1: Technology Serves People
Technology is an instrument for human flourishing, agency, and reach—never an end in itself. Software must adapt to human needs, protect human focus, and serve organizational clarity.

---

### Principle 2: Human Authority Is Final
Human Principals retain absolute, unchallengeable authority over all synthetic agents, workflows, and corporate outputs. No algorithm, AI model, or automated heuristic may overrule a human Principal's explicit direction.

---

### Principle 3: AI Augments Capability
Artificial intelligence exists to amplify human cognitive reach, handle routine execution drag, and provide rigorous independent analysis. AI is a partner in execution, not a replacement for human strategic judgment.

---

### Principle 4: Privacy Is Fundamental
Data privacy is a non-negotiable human right and enterprise imperative. Personal and corporate intelligence belongs exclusively to the user. Egress outside local storage requires explicit, policy-signed permission (**INV-05**).

---

### Principle 5: Simplicity Over Complexity
Elegance is achieved when there is nothing left to remove. We reject unnecessary features, complex configuration interfaces, and visual bloat in favor of quiet, predictable, high-precision execution.

---

### Principle 6: Trust Before Features
High-trust output (>95% verification rate) takes precedence over feature volume. We will never ship a feature that degrades system stability, auditability, or output verification trust.

---

### Principle 7: Attention Is Sacred (INV-01)
Human cognitive attention is the rarest resource in modern enterprise leadership. Software must earn the right to disturb a user. Unsolicited popups, decorative badges, and notification clutter are strictly prohibited.

---

### Principle 8: Long-Term Thinking Over Short-Term Hacks
We build for decades, not quarterly hype cycles. Data formats, system architectures, and documentation structures are designed for multi-decade durability and backward compatibility.

---

### Principle 9: Sustainable & Responsible Evolution
Product capabilities evolve systematically through disciplined governance, formal ADRs, and thorough testing. We reject chaotic breaking changes and unvetted rapid mutations.

---

### Principle 10: Responsible & Audited AI (INV-02)
Synthetic agents operate under strict separation of powers. Author agents can never audit or approve their own work. All AI executions are bounded by hard financial caps and capability fences.

---

### Principle 11: Local-First By Default (INV-04)
Core workspace memory, vector indices, and event ledgers reside locally on user hardware. The cloud is strictly an optional, policy-guarded burst layer—never a structural dependency.

---

### Principle 12: Radical Transparency Over Hidden Behavior (INV-03)
System state, permission checks, model routing calculations, and audit logs are fully legible and cryptographically signed. Zero hidden background data harvesting or undisclosed agent operations.
